islands.csv and island_names.csv - Kaafu Connect data pack
===========================================================

What they are
-------------
islands.csv       One row per island the trip calculator uses: 27 rows.
island_names.csv  Every spelling of those islands seen in our sources,
                  mapped to one island_key: 172 rows. Use it to fix names
                  before joining anything.

Both built 23 Oct 2026. UTF-8 with a BOM, so Excel shows é correctly.


The one rule: join on island_key
--------------------------------
island_key is RTL style: atoll code, dot, island name, no space.
  K.Maafushi   AA.Rasdhoo   ADh.Dhangethi   V.Felidhoo   K.Malé

Never join on the island name alone. Three of our islands share a name with
an island in another atoll:
  V.Thinadhoo   (258 people)  vs  GDh. Thinadhoo (6,217 people)
  K.Guraidhoo                 vs  Th. Guraidhoo
  ADh.Omadhoo                 vs  Th. Omadhoo
A name-only join would give Vaavu's Thinadhoo 24 times its real population.

Atoll codes: K = Kaafu, AA = Alif Alif, ADh = Alif Dhaalu, V = Vaavu.

islands.csv: columns
--------------------
The first eight are the columns from the prep guide; the rest are extras.

  island_key               Join key (see above)
  atoll                    K, AA, ADh, V
  island                   Island name, our spelling
  harbour_lat, harbour_lon Harbour point, decimal degrees (WGS84)
  population_2022          Census 2022 total resident population, incl.
                           foreign residents
  census_spelling          Name(s) as written in observations.csv. Malé and
                           Hulhumalé are sums of several Census rows.
  has_health_centre        yes if facilities.csv has a hospital or health
                           centre on the island
  atoll_name               Kaafu, Alif Alif, Alif Dhaalu, Vaavu
  role                     origin, greater_male, transfer
  foreign_population_2022  Foreign residents (already inside
                           population_2022). High on guesthouse islands,
                           e.g. Maafushi 1,276 of 4,471.
  harbour_source           odi_connections, or the OSM feature used
  harbour_quality          island_point = copied from odi_connections; this
                           is the island's centre, not its harbour (see
                           below);
                           exact = mapped harbour or ferry terminal
  health_facility_ids      Matching facility_id(s) in facilities.csv
  has_hospital             yes for Rasdhoo, Mahibadhoo, Felidhoo (atoll
                           hospitals) and Greater Malé
  health_location_quality  location_quality of those facilities
  odi_name                 The island's name in odi_connections.csv
  notes                    Spelling differences and name clashes


Where the numbers come from
---------------------------
- Population: Team Kandu observations.csv, metric census_population and
  census_foreign_population, date 2022. Original source: MBS Statistical
  Yearbook 2026, table 3.3 (Census 2022). The "Republic" row there is
  515,132, which matches the published Census total including foreigners.
- Malé = Galolhu + Henveiru + Maafannu + Machchangolhi wards (137,238).
  The Census "Maale" row (211,908) is the whole city including Hulhumalé,
  Villimalé and Hulhulé, so do not use it for the island of Malé.
- Hulhumalé = Hulhumaale Phase 1 + Phase 2 (65,714).
- Harbour points, 5 real ones:
    K.Gaafaru           harbour area                  OSM way 941025096
    K.Guraidhoo         ferry terminal                OSM node 5833834250
    ADh.Hangnaameedhoo  harbour "AD. Hangnaameedhoo"  OSM way 150655824
    K.Villimalé         RTL/MTCC ferry terminal       OSM node 4462445840
    K.Hulhumalé         Hulhumalé Ferry Terminal      rtl_bus_stops, stop 507
- The other 22 come from odi_connections.csv, and they are island centres,
  not harbours. Checked 23 Oct against OSM: most sit within 20-200 m of
  OSM's island point and 100-300 m from the nearest pier, breakwater or
  ferry terminal. Dhigurah is a long island and its point is 1.4 km from
  OSM's island point.
  What it means:
    Outer islands: the walk to the health centre may be off by a few
      minutes. Fine for the demo.
    K.Malé: the point is central Malé, not a ferry terminal. Kaafu ferries
      and speedboats arrive at different Malé jetties, so member 3 should
      record the arrival terminal on each ferry_links row. The bus or car
      leg then starts from that terminal (Malé terminals in rtl_bus_stops
      include Maafannu Ferry Terminal, code 1101, and Jetty NO5, code 1008).
  To make a point exact, click the island's main harbour in OSM or Google
  Maps, replace harbour_lat/lon, and set harbour_quality to exact.
- Built with an AI-assisted script outside the project repo. Mention it
  under "AI-assisted parts" in the README.


island_names.csv: how to use it
-------------------------------
Columns: variant, island_key, seen_in.

For each island it lists the key, the RTL style with a space ("ADh.
Dhangethi"), the odi_connections name ("Adh. Dhangethi"), the Census name
("ADh Dhagethi"), the plain name, and other spellings found (Dhagethi,
Hangnameedhoo, Hura, Male', Maale, Hulhumaale, Vilimaale, Villingili,
Velana International Airport, VIA, ...).

When member 3 types ferry_links, or anyone brings in a new file:
  1. Put the name column next to a new island_key column.
  2. In Excel: =XLOOKUP(A2, island_names!A:A, island_names!B:B, "NOT FOUND")
  3. Fix every NOT FOUND by hand, and add the new spelling to
     island_names.csv so it is caught next time.
Plain names like "Thinadhoo" map to our V.Thinadhoo only because the other
Thinadhoo is not in our project. Prefer variants that include the atoll.


Checks already run
------------------
- Every island_key in facilities.csv exists in islands.csv.
- Every island name in odi_connections.csv maps to a row in islands.csv.
- No spelling in island_names.csv points to two different keys.
- Population totals: Kaafu 17,749, inbound 13,665, all origins 31,414.
To repeat the facilities check in Excel, in facilities.csv:
  =COUNTIF(islands!A:A, E2)   (E = island_key; any 0 is a broken join)
