hdx_benchmark.csv - Kaafu Connect data pack
===========================================
HDX Maldives accessibility indicators, hospitals, car-only travel time
(file MDV_hospitals_access_wide.csv). Share of population within 30, 60,
90 and 120 minutes, for the Maldives (ADM0), Kaafu, Malé and Alif Alif
(ADM1). HDX has no row for Alif Dhaalu or Vaavu, and nothing beyond 120
min, so the 240-minute band has no HDX value.

Headline: Kaafu 77.41% within 2 h, Maldives 45.89%.
HDX uses its own modelled population (Kaafu ~7,770), not Census 2022
(17,749), so compare percentages, not head counts.
Credit: HDX / Maldives accessibility indicators dataset.
