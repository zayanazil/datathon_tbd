bus_service_hours.csv and bus_headways.csv - Kaafu Connect data pack
====================================================================

What they are
-------------
bus_service_hours.csv  One row per Greater Malé bus route x day group:
                       first and last departure. 45 rows (15 routes x 3).
bus_headways.csv       The published frequency bands behind those times
                       ("06:00-08:00 every 15 min"). 117 rows.
Built 23 Oct 2026 from the MTCC/RTL route timetable PDFs (R1-R15).
UTF-8 with a BOM.

Day groups: weekday = Sun-Thu, saturday = Sat and public holidays,
friday = Fri. The build day is Wednesday, so use day_type = weekday.

bus_service_hours.csv columns
-----------------------------
  route_number, route_name, first_stop   as in rtl_bus_routes.csv
  days, day_type                         see above
  first_departure, last_departure        HH:MM from the first stop
  first_min, last_min                    minutes from 00:00; after midnight
                                         is > 1440 (00:30 = 1470)
  trips_per_day                          departures counted from the bands
  trips_per_day_rtl                      the RTL app's count, for comparison
  breaks                                 gaps over 90 min (Friday prayers)
  in_build_plan                          yes for the 11 timed routes in
                                         BUILD_PLAN; "no (minibus)" for R10,
                                         R11, R12, R15, which now also have
                                         times and can be added if wanted
  estimated, source, checked_on, notes

bus_headways.csv columns
------------------------
  route_number, days, day_type, band_start, band_end (start times, end
  inclusive), start_min, end_min, every_min, wait_min (= every_min / 2),
  estimated, source, notes

Use for the wait: BUILD_PLAN says wait = half the headway, with headway =
(last - first) / trips_per_day. bus_headways.csv gives the real headway by
time of day (e.g. R1 is every 15 min at 07:00 but every 30 at 23:00), so
wait_min for the band containing the arrival time is better. Either works.

Things to know
--------------
1. R3, R4, R5, R6: only the RAMADAN 2024 timetable was supplied. Iftar
   break removed; first departures taken from it; evenings checked
   against RTL live departures (fetched Sat 10 Oct 2026). Marked
   estimated = yes.
     R5: the Ramadan table (every 30 min, 07:00-17:00) doesn't match RTL
     live data (hourly at :15, 17 trips a day), so hourly 06:15-22:15 is
     used.
2. R10, R11, R12, R14: the PDFs have no Saturday table; Sun-Thu times
   assumed for Saturday (estimated = yes on those rows).
3. R13 runs 24 hours (every 20-30 min overnight).
4. Airport routes: times are from the town end (R3 West Park, R4 HM
   Phase 1, R9 HM Phase 2); the airport end leaves 30-35 min later.
5. Cross-check against RTL live data (fetched on a Saturday): every
   evening departure in rtl_bus_departures.csv fits these bands, and the
   Saturday trip counts match RTL's trips_per_day within 4 for every route
   except R13 (120 vs 92; unclear what RTL counts for a 24-hour route).
   R6 Saturday uses every 15 min, not the Ramadan table's 20, to match.
6. The non-Ramadan PDFs carry no effective date.
